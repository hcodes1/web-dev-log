Grouping Content with <div> and <span>

The `<div>` and `<span>` elements are generic containers used for grouping content. They don't have inherent semantic meaning but are crucial for applying styles or JavaScript to specific sections of content.

Using <div> for Block-Level Grouping

A `<div>` is a block-level element. This means it will always start on a new line and take up the full available width, acting like a distinct block of content on the page. It's ideal for grouping larger sections or components.

Using <span> for Inline-Level Grouping

A `<span>` is an inline-level element. This means it does *not* start on a new line and only takes up as much width as its content requires. It's perfect for grouping small phrases or words within a line of text.
